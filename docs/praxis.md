# Praxis | Field operations and learning prototype

## Intent
Praxis is designed as a broader, technician-first collection of tools, not only a chiller training app. Its concepts include field documentation, site orientation, navigation context, checklists, travel administration and learning.

**Praxis Chiller Academy** is a separate chiller-focused educational module within that broader vision.

## Current prototype ideas
- Labeled photo evidence and technician notes.
- QA and service workflows, with equipment context.
- Training and instructor-controlled lessons.
- Safety prompts and realistic lab checkpoints.
- Manual exports/handoffs rather than pretending an unapproved external integration works.

## Boundaries
The development preview is not a live system of record, not an OEM-approved technical procedure, and not proof of ServiceMax integration, employer approval, or customer-data access.

Some development and preview environments remain deliberately protected. This public overview publishes **no confidential asset lists, site maps, work orders, credentials, or client information.**

## Next evidence milestone
Produce a controlled demonstration using synthetic work-order and equipment data, document device test results, and publish only after checking data handling and authorization.
