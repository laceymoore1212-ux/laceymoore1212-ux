# RIFT | Research traceability and verification framework

RIFT is an evolving approach to researching technical subjects with AI while preserving the distinction between documented facts, hypotheses, and unverified assumptions.

## Goals
- Collect source references and record their provenance.
- Keep manufacturer/model distinctions explicit.
- Identify knowledge gaps before writing instructions.
- Distinguish generic education from procedures that require an OEM document or instructor.
- Use documented test cases and revision history to improve repeatability across models.

## Application
One research case evaluates chiller compressor teardown and inspection teaching materials for Praxis Chiller Academy. It uses a generic educational checklist as a starting point rather than pretending one checklist is safe for every model.

## Limitations
The framework is a **research prototype**, not an independent certification, manufacturer-approved service procedure, or substitute for instructor supervision, LOTO, electrical safety or applicable IOMs.
